# AI SEO Content Production Playbook

## 1. Overview

This playbook outlines a structured approach to creating AI-powered SEO content based on research from industry experts.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step SOP

### Step 1: Topic Selection

Identify low-competition, high-intent topics using keyword research.

(Source: Brian Dean, YouTube video – SEO Strategy, accessed 2026)

---

### Step 2: AI Content Creation

Use AI tools to generate outlines and drafts, but refine manually for clarity and originality.

(Source: Julian Goldie, YouTube video – AI SEO content creation, accessed 2026)

---

### Step 3: Content Structuring

Write clear, answer-first, and scannable content using headings and bullet points.

(Source: Kevin Indig, LinkedIn post, accessed 2026)

---

### Step 4: Optimization

Optimize content using keywords, internal links, and topical authority.

(Source: Ahrefs, YouTube video, accessed 2026)

---

### Step 5: Distribution

Promote content across platforms like LinkedIn to increase reach.

(Source: Ross Simmonds, LinkedIn post, accessed 2026)

---

### Step 6: Authority Building

Build trust through consistent, high-quality content and brand presence.

(Source: Lily Ray, LinkedIn post, accessed 2026)
